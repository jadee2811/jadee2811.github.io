Designing the workflow before choosing the tool

A practical method for finding the decision, handoff and exception a product needs to serve.

By Jade · Jade Share
TABLE OF CONTENTS01 Start with the decision02 Map the handoff03 Scale the system

These notes are a framework for discussing product and AI delivery. They do not represent a claim about a single client outcome.

Start with the recurring decision

Look first at the repeated decision a team makes, the context it requires and the consequence of getting it wrong. This reveals the useful unit of work before a feature or tool enters the discussion.

Map the handoffs

Document where information changes hands, where it is lost and where someone must review it. Clear handoffs make a workflow easier to evaluate and improve.

Useful systems are defined by the workflow around a decision, not only by the quality of a single response.

Scale the system, not the demo

Scaling changes ownership, data, training and exception handling. A sustainable plan keeps those conditions visible from the start.
