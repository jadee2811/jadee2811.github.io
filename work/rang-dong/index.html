Rạng Đông: an AI assistant supporting dealers through the purchasing journey

An AI assistant that helps dealers look up products and place orders, with clear commercial handoffs.

Client
Rạng Đông
Role
Product Owner
Year
2024–2026

1. Context and problem

Dealers need to look up products, stock and policies and place orders quickly, while the sales team cannot answer every repeated question.

  • The product catalog is large with many technical specifications.
  • Dealers need fast, consistent responses.
  • Commercial requests must be routed to the right person.

2. Role

I was Product Owner: clarifying dealer support scenarios, organizing the product knowledge base, and designing conversation flows and handoff rules for staff.

3. Approach

  1. 01
    Context-aware supportAnswers are based on the product and situation the dealer is asking about.
  2. 02
    Predictable commercial handoffQuotes, discounts and special orders go to the right department.
  3. 03
    Measured by operational metricsTrack response time and satisfaction.
93.8%CSAT
8.927 secondsfirst response time (FRT)

4. Lessons

  • In B2B, answering in the dealer's context matters more than answering a lot.
  • The boundary between AI and the sales team must be clear from the start.
