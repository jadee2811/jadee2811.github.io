Money Forward Vietnam: candidate experience and employer brand

A digital experience for candidates, built around the hiring journey and the employer brand of a fintech company.

Client
Money Forward Vietnam
Role
UX Content Lead
Year
2022–2024

1. Context and problem

Money Forward Vietnam needed a place where candidates could understand what the company does, what its culture is like and which role suits them, before deciding to apply.

  • Candidates need clear information about roles, locations and the hiring process.
  • The employer brand must be consistent with the product brand.
  • Content must be easy to update as hiring needs change.

2. Role

I led content and experience: information structure, brand storytelling, and the writing for each step of the candidate journey, working with the client's design, engineering and HR teams.

3. Approach

  1. 01
    Follow the candidate journeyFrom learning about the company and browsing roles to submitting an application.
  2. 02
    One brand storyA consistent voice and message across all pages.
  3. 03
    Content that can be operatedJob pages structured so the HR team can update them on its own.

4. Lessons

  • For candidates, clarity matters more than polish.
  • The employer brand is part of the product and should be designed as a journey.
