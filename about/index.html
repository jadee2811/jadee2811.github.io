Someone of many trades, living on Earth fully.

Jade, Bích Ngọc: curious since childhood, now an AI Product Owner who still wants to experience life as fully and vividly as possible.

As a kid I read a hundred thousand “whys” and I never really stopped asking.

Growing up, I liked everything: books, drawing, animals, sport, cameras, the stars. I could be a person of many trades, yet what I want most is simple: to live this life on Earth as fully and vividly as I can.

I try to stay kind and easy with everyone, and to give my all and savour every moment. Work is one part of life, something I treat like a hobby but not the whole of it. And when I do it, I do it wholeheartedly.

All of it, gathered together, is Jade. Is Bích Ngọc.
